## Name Origin

- **Pronunciation:** REE-oh
- **Style:** Modern, short, global
- **Feel:** Friendly, approachable, memorable

While "Rio" literally means "river" in Spanish/Portuguese, for Mehfooze it represents:

- **Flow** — Always moving, always watching
- **Adaptability** — Rivers find a way, so does Rio
- **Life** — Rivers sustain life; Rio protects it

---

## How Rio Represents the Brand

### 1. Protection Through Presence
Rio is a ghost — always there, even when unseen. Like a guardian spirit watching over users.

### 2. Friendly Safety
Rio makes safety feel approachable, not scary. A cute ghost = protection without fear.

### 3. Guiding Spirit
Rio appears during first-time onboarding to guide users through the app's features.

### 4. Consistent Identity
Rio unifies the app and web experience as the same companion across platforms.
